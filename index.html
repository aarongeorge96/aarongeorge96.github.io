Coffee Sales Dashboard

Are customers coming back, and which products actually make money?

Built an interactive Excel dashboard for an online coffee bean seller, then checked every figure in Python and went further into profit, repeat customers and the loyalty card.

  • $45.1Ktotal sales
  • 10.0%profit margin
  • 2.7%repeat customers
  • Only 25 of 913 customers ordered more than once, so winning the second order is the biggest opportunity.
  • The loyalty card makes no difference: holders return at almost the same rate and spend less per order.
  • Liberica earns the most profit at a 13% margin, while Robusta earns just 6%.
